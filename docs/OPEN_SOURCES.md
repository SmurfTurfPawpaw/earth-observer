# Open sources for journalism and economic context

Reviewed **11 October 2026**. This is a shortlist for integration planning, not a claim that every listed service is already enabled. Existing intelligence integrations are described in [`INTELLIGENCE_SOURCES.md`](INTELLIGENCE_SOURCES.md); existing globe-layer clearances are in [`DATA_POLICY.md`](DATA_POLICY.md).

## Sources with a practical open-data path

| Source | Useful scope | Reuse basis and exceptions | Access and implementation conditions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **GDELT DOC 2.0** | Multilingual article discovery by topic, country mention, and time; coverage links | GDELT datasets permit unrestricted academic, commercial, and government use with GDELT citation/link. Publisher bodies, photographs, and third-party maps do not inherit that permission. [Terms](https://www.gdeltproject.org/about.html#termsofuse) | Public JSON; official documentation gives an ArticleList maximum of 250. Bound each query and time range, cache globally, coalesce identical requests, and honor failure backoff. `seendate` should be labeled indexed, not assumed to be original publication time. Coverage volume and sentiment describe media coverage, not incident truth. [API documentation](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/) |
| **World Bank Indicators v2** | Comparable population, debt, industry, poverty, trade, and development context | Default CC BY 4.0 for data, with World Bank/data-provider attribution and additional terms. Individual series may carry third-party restrictions; check indicator/source metadata before redistributing. [Data terms](https://data.worldbank.org/summary-terms-of-use) | No API key required. Use explicit series and source IDs, paginate results, preserve units and observation periods, and distinguish missing values from zero. These are generally lagged statistics, not live tension events. Refresh on release schedules rather than every user's page load. [API overview](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/889392), [query/pagination documentation](https://datahelpdesk.worldbank.org/knowledgebase/articles/898581) |
| **Eurostat** | EU trade, production, energy, prices, population, and employment | Statistical data reuse for commercial/non-commercial purposes is authorized with attribution. Check dataset notices and third-party exceptions; identify transformations and the required Eurostat non-responsibility statement. [Reuse policy](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/help/copyright-notice) | Official dissemination APIs and bulk files are suitable for curated country/sector series. Preserve dataset code, access date, units, frequency, seasonal-adjustment status, and observation flags; compare equivalent series rather than raw unlike values. Use a server cache and release-based updates. [API entry point](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/web/user-guides/data-browser/api-data-access/api-getting-started) |
| **BLS Public Data API** | US employment, prices, wages, and productivity benchmarks | BLS-published material is public domain except previously copyrighted photographs/illustrations; cite BLS. Its emblem is protected. [Copyright guidance](https://www.bls.gov/bls/linksite.htm) | Unregistered access: 25 queries/day, 25 series/query, 10 years/query. Registered v2: 500/day, 50 series, 20 years, annual key renewal. Both list 50 requests/10 seconds; a small cached app should run well below that ceiling. Use known series IDs and release schedules, store revisions, and label annualized versus year-over-year growth. [Limits](https://www.bls.gov/developers/api_faqs.htm), [v2 signatures](https://www.bls.gov/developers/api_signature_v2.htm) |
| **EIA Open Data / official energy releases** | Oil, gas, electricity, and energy-related supply/demand context | EIA-produced data and information products may be reused with dated source acknowledgment. Privately licensed material and photographs are exceptions; EIA trademarks are excluded. [Copyright and reuse](https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php) | API v2 uses a key, normally kept server-side. Query specified routes, facets, frequency and data fields; JSON results are capped at 5,000 rows and require pagination beyond that. Per-second/hour throttles apply; do not crawl millions of series or poll lagged observations as if live. [Technical documentation](https://www.eia.gov/opendata/documentation.php) |
| **USGS minerals, hazards, and agency datasets** | Mineral production/reserves, resource geography, earthquakes; evidence alongside industrial stories | USGS-produced information is generally public domain; credited third-party materials have separate rights. Preserve attribution, publication/version, and observation year. [USGS policy](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits) | Hazard feeds provide current observations; mineral tables are periodic and revised. Map only appropriate precision and carry source IDs. A resource deposit is not equivalent to operating refining capacity. Useful entry: [Mineral Commodity Summaries](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/national-minerals-information-center/mineral-commodity-summaries). |
| **OFAC Sanctions List Service** | Official US sanctions designations, names, aliases, programs, and available vessel identifiers | Use agency-authored downloadable list facts and primary notices with source attribution; preserve jurisdiction and version. Access does not license private photographs or third-party attachments, nor turn a designation into a court finding. [Official list-service guidance](https://ofac.treasury.gov/other-ofac-sanctions-lists) | Use supported SLS downloads/API; legacy redirected download URLs can return 403. Ingest authoritative additions/removals and timestamps. Exact identifiers such as IMO numbers are better joins than fuzzy names; failed matching must not create a public accusation. A list entry is not an individual vessel's real-time position. [Endpoint migration notice](https://ofac.treasury.gov/sdn-list-data-formats-data-schemas/ofac-technical-actions-in-reverse-chronological-order/20240516_44) |
| **UCDP GED and Candidate datasets** | Versioned records of organized violence, with documented definitions and location/date/fatality uncertainty | The UCDP data-use FAQ states CC BY 4.0 except otherwise noted. Use the dataset's required citation and inspect the relevant release/codebook for exceptions. [Official terms/citation FAQ](https://www.uu.se/en/department/peace-and-conflict-research/research/ucdp/frequently-asked-questions) | Downloads offer a vetted batch-data path. The API now requires a token requested from its maintainer, with a documented 5,000-request daily limit including errors. No unauthorized keyless fallback. Candidate data are provisional, not real-time confirmed incidents; preserve uncertainty and dataset versions. [Downloads](https://ucdp.uu.se/downloads/), [current API requirements](https://ucdp.uu.se/apidocs/) |

The rights descriptions above apply to the specified material and use. “No fee,” “government website,” “RSS,” or “public API” alone is not a blanket redistribution license. Record the exact dataset and its notice before enabling a new provider.

## Useful sources that require a narrower or approved path

| Source | Suitable use | Condition before enabling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ReliefWeb API** | Humanitarian report discovery, original-source links, countries, and disaster categories | Since 1 November 2025, a pre-approved `appname` is required. The API permits 1,000 calls/day and 1,000 returned entries/call, and notes that partner reports may retain original copyright. Obtain the approved name, keep a link/metadata scope, and inspect rights before copying report text or attachments. [Parameters](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/parameters), [terms and quotas](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/) |
| **IEA open reports/commentaries** | Primary energy/critical-mineral context and explicitly open material | Default CC BY 4.0 covers specified IEA-produced text/reports/figures, with exceptions. Oil Market Report, standalone databases/data explorers, data annexes, joint works, and third-party content are not covered by that default. Do not turn the open-report policy into a blanket “free IEA data API” assumption. [IEA terms](https://www.iea.org/terms) |
| **YouTube / Maxinomics** | Linked creator commentary, embedded official playback, curated metadata and brief original analysis | Obtain Data API credentials for a maintained inventory and obey current developer/storage policies. Caption download requires video-edit permission; a public video is not permission to publish a transcript corpus. Creator-approved transcripts are the reliable route for complete reuse. [Caption requirements](https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/captions/download), [developer policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies?hl=en) |
| **ACLED** | Potentially useful conflict analysis after a suitable agreement | Not a default open public event feed. Current EULA restricts commercial entities without corporate licensing and external data products must be transformative and not recreate underlying records. Merely displaying records in a dashboard is insufficient. Keep excluded until the intended access/display is licensed; the content usage terms also contain AI/ML restrictions. [EULA](https://acleddata.com/eula), [content usage](https://acleddata.com/contentusage) |
| **Publisher RSS, social posts, commercial AIS, paid imagery, freight indices** | Discovery links or independently licensed context | Evaluate each provider's actual permission, access method, data type, and redistribution conditions. RSS accessibility, a social embed, or a chart in a video does not clear the underlying article, image, vessel feed, or time series. Do not use user cookies, arbitrary proxy endpoints, or third-party reposts as production fallbacks. |

## Data model and operating choices

Keep one provenance record per source item: stable ID, provider, original URL, creator/agency, observation and publication dates where supplied, retrieval time, category, geography and precision, source type, uncertainty/revision status, license notice, and whether the presentation is a measurement, report, allegation, commentary, or forecast. Preserve unknown values instead of inventing coordinates or timestamps.

Use a small provider registry with fixed upstream hosts and explicit routes. Poll once per server cache interval, coalesce concurrent requests, limit bodies/result counts, honor `Retry-After`, and keep bounded stale data only with its age visible. Requested credentials and approved application names are configuration prerequisites, not browser-visible defaults. Economic series should follow releases; article discovery can refresh in minutes when visible; versioned conflict datasets belong on their actual release cycle.

For related coverage, retain each item and use grouping as navigation. Independent confirmation requires checking whether the sources have different evidence, not just different domains. Country search hits are about that country name; they do not establish an incident's location. Theme labels derived from search are discovery categories and must not be promoted into facts or numerical tension scores.

The immediate editorial foundation can combine article discovery with official hazards and primary economic context, then add approved humanitarian/conflict feeds and validated country dossiers. Keep research snapshots, real-time measurements, and machine-discovered coverage clearly dated and separately identified.
