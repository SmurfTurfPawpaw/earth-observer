Their earlier EUR5/month total project ceiling has not been replaced. Account access, an X-specific budget, account-approved analysis scope and commercial/display rights remain unresolved.

The official pricing page currently lists USD0.005 per post read and USD0.010 per trends request. Filtered streams match selected rules; they do not establish complete X coverage. Source: https://docs.x.com/x-api/getting-started/pricing and https://docs.x.com/x-api/posts/filtered-stream/introduction .

The developer console redirected to sign-in; existing API entitlement could not be verified. No account settings, purchases, credentials or paid API calls were made. A proposed dormant adapter was held before files were created. Its budget interface was a design proposal, not an implemented durable ledger.

X's agreement restricts using content or derived analysis to target or serve off-platform advertising, and requires approved analysis scope. Larger commercial use and post display/storage require further clearance. Source: https://docs.x.com/developer-terms/agreement . Do not infer that anonymous sponsorship automatically resolves those restrictions.

Cheaper candidates under evaluation: GDELT metadata, permitted publisher feeds, official event/statistical feeds, and a bounded Bluesky/AT Protocol social stream. GDELT permits commercial dataset reuse with attribution; publisher content has separate rights. Bluesky public stream access does not transfer authors' content rights or remove processing/storage costs. Grok X Search also bills per fetched item plus model usage; it is not a verified cheaper X substitute.

Source selection remains pending the user's response to alternatives. Keep the separate design preview's sample data identified. Do not activate paid collection, billing, a new source pipeline, or sponsor targeting based on this record. Source coverage, deletions/edits, event locations, uncertainty and history must be explicit contracts before integration. Preserve existing unrelated backend work.
