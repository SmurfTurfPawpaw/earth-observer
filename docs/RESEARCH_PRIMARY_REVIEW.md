# Dated primary-source review of the Maxinomics synthesis

Checked **11 October 2026**. This supplements the [existing synthesis](MAXINOMICS_RESEARCH.md#representative-primary-source-checks) and [video inventory](../public/research/maxinomics.json); it does not change their review classifications. The checks below use official government, statistical-agency, and intergovernmental sources. They distinguish a dated measurement or legal instrument from the creator's interpretation.

## 1. Rare-earth concentration and export-control leverage

**Existing claim:** processing and magnet-making capabilities can provide geopolitical leverage. Relevant video: [China Found Something Better Than Oil](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BXLGV0Sj0n8), published 11 September 2026; existing coverage is an unverified third-party full-text repost.

The IEA's 2026 report supports strong concentration, but its figures describe **2024 magnet rare earths**, rather than all minerals or current October 2026 shares: China supplied 60% of mined production, 91% of refined output, and 94% of sintered permanent magnets. Each percentage has a different production-stage denominator. The report also describes recovered exports after licensing resumed. [IEA, Rare Earth Elements executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/rare-earth-elements/executive-summary).

The exact legal timing sharpens the existing caveat. China's **7 November 2025 announcement 70** suspends announcements 55–58 and 61–62 until **10 November 2026**. The Chinese original names those instruments; it does not name April announcement 18. The latter introduced licensing for specified medium/heavy rare-earth items, effective 4 April 2025. **Inference:** the suspension of those October measures is not evidence that the April licensing requirements were also suspended. [MOFCOM/Customs announcement 70](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/blgg/gg/2025/art/2025/art_da147202d0934ddebe252772e95ed7b3.html), [announcement 18](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/blgg/gg/2025/art/2025/art_5e663d0bfec7452dabd950d0f0e42153.html). Dates and scope here are an English paraphrase of the Chinese originals.

The IEA's USD 6.5 trillion annual downstream-production figure is **conditional exposure under full implementation**, not measured losses. Its 2026 outlook also records a modest 2025 reduction in rare-earth refining concentration from US/Malaysian capacity. [IEA, Global Critical Minerals Outlook 2026 executive summary](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-critical-minerals-outlook-2026/executive-summary).

**Editorial recommendation:** retain the processing-bottleneck theme, but attach the stage, statistical year, and named legal instrument to every factual card. Describe bargaining leverage as an interpretation. Recheck announcement 70 before stating that its suspension has ended; this review establishes the published deadline, not what a future government decision will be.

## 2. Iranian oil sanctions and the meaning of “nobody”

**Existing claim:** restrictions and maritime practices shape Iranian oil trade; the video's title is not a universal legal prohibition. Relevant video: [The Oil Nobody Is Allowed to Buy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IWj39ekjwcE), published **31 March 2026**. Only its official description was reviewed, so this check addresses the title and existing summary, not a claim attributed to an unread transcript.

There was a material exception at that publication date. **OFAC General License U** authorized specified sale, delivery, and offloading transactions for Iranian-origin crude/petroleum loaded on vessels by **12:01 a.m. EDT, 20 March 2026**, through **12:01 a.m. EDT, 19 April 2026**, subject to its exclusions. **Inference:** the March 31 title cannot be read literally as a universal ban even within the listed US authorities. The authorization covered particular cargo and transactions; it did not remove sanctions generally or decide other jurisdictions' rules. [OFAC General License U](https://ofac.treasury.gov/system/files/2026-03/iran_glu_0.pdf).

The later license cited in the existing synthesis also needs an expiry. On **7 July 2026**, GL X1 revoked/superseded GL X and allowed specified wind-down transactions only through **12:01 a.m. EDT, 17 July 2026**. It generally excluded new purchases or loading from July 7, except where necessary for that wind-down. It is historical context, not evidence of a current October authorization. [OFAC July 7 action](https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20260707), [General License X1, paragraphs (a)–(c)](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/936446/download?inline=).

The Treasury alert dated **28 April 2026** estimates that China purchased approximately 90% of Iranian oil exports. Preserve the agency attribution and date: this is not an independently audited October market share or a continuous trade series. [US Treasury teapot-refinery alert](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0476).

**Editorial recommendation:** add GL U as publication-date context and mark GL X1 as expired. A current sanctions explainer must check the relevant jurisdiction, entities, cargo dates, and applicable instrument afresh. This bounded review does not determine eligibility for a particular transaction or exhaust every current license.

## 3. Productivity, AI causation, and household prosperity

**Existing claim:** freight and investment signals suggest an optimistic growth scenario, but aggregate productivity does not establish AI causation. Relevant video: [Americans Are About to Get a Lot Richer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mCd9ctg1sO4), published **29 May 2026**; existing coverage is an unverified third-party full-text repost.

The **3 September 2026 revised Q2 release** confirms nonfarm-business productivity growth of **1.4% quarter-to-quarter at a seasonally adjusted annualized rate**, and **2.2% from Q2 2025**. Productivity is real output per hour worked. These are distinct comparisons, and the release does not identify an AI contribution. It also reports real hourly compensation down 3.3% at the quarterly annualized rate and 0.1% over four quarters. **Inference:** output-per-hour growth alone cannot substantiate a general household-income or wealth forecast. [BLS archived Q2 release and technical notes](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/prod2_09032026.htm).

At this check, the [current BLS release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/prod2.nr0.htm) still displayed the same September 3 release and figures. The fixed archive is the reproducible citation. **Temporal correction:** September's revised Q2 data were published after the May 29 video; they are later context, not contemporaneous evidence the creator could have cited on that date.

**Editorial recommendation:** keep the two reported measures with their periods and annualization stated. Present AI causation and future prosperity as hypotheses requiring separate evidence on investment, output, wages, household distribution, and timing. The existing numeric check is correct; its remaining limit is what those statistics can explain.

## Scope of the result

These three checks reinforce the synthesis's existing caution and add precise temporal context. They do not validate every figure in the reviewed reposts, the unresolved Shorts, predictions of geopolitical outcomes, or the completeness of the transcript corpus. The reference-card module described below displays this limited review; it adds no transcript coverage and does not change the original inventory or publish a release.

## Structured reference cards

[`primary-context.json`](../public/research/primary-context.json) contains three records under `schemaVersion: 1`, with a UTC `reviewedAt`. Each record has a stable `id`, short `title`, `topic`, country codes, a narrow `finding` marked `primary-record-paraphrase`, and dated `sources` joined by `sourceIds`. A source's `dateKind` distinguishes issue, document, effective, and release dates; translated or shortened titles are labeled. `temporalScope` preserves historical periods and expired authorizations. `relationToCreator.kind` is always `inference`; `relatedCreator` retains the video date and actual review coverage. Country codes describe jurisdictions or subjects, never incident coordinates. These cards contain no full transcript, confidence score, trading recommendation, or added video-review claim. Before displaying them, retain their dates, limitations, official links, and inference labels; this file's creation does not make them a live feed.

## Display and acquisition contract

[`primary-context.js`](../src/core/primary-context.js) exports `createPrimaryContextPanel(container, { fetchImpl, documentRef, url, origin, now, store })`, returning `open()`, `close()`, `setFilters({ q, category, country })`, and `destroy()`. Mount it in a separate compact Research context disclosure before the live report cards. Call `open()` when the news panel opens, `close()` when it closes, and `destroy()` during teardown. Creating the component and changing filters perform no requests. Successful metadata remains in session memory; there is no polling. Closing or hiding the panel cancels acquisition and rejects late responses. A visible panel may resume a canceled first acquisition.

The optional `url` must be a fixed same-origin `research/primary-context.json` path, such as `/earth/research/primary-context.json`; it cannot carry query parameters, a fragment, credentials, or another origin. The loader requests JSON without credentials, rejects redirects, and caps decoded bytes at 64 KiB. It validates the exact schema, reviewed source links, date kinds, creator coverage, bounded plain text, and source identifiers. Future review, source, or creator-publication dates are invalid; a future deadline explicitly published in a dated legal instrument is permitted and is never presented as a prediction. Direct DOM-helper callers receive the same validation, and text is rendered through `textContent`.

An optional browser store supplies a last-good device copy after acquisition fails. Its retrieval timestamp must be no more than seven days old and cannot precede the review. Storage failure cannot invalidate a successfully checked response. A device copy remains labeled as saved metadata, retains the original review and source dates, and is not evidence of a new source review. Filters operate locally on the small reference set, with countries interpreted as jurisdictions or subjects. These records do not enter live-feed grouping, discovery ranking, geographic incidents, or query-demand counts.

The module pass reopened only the five official URLs present in this JSON: MOFCOM announcements [70](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/blgg/gg/2025/art/2025/art_da147202d0934ddebe252772e95ed7b3.html) and [18](https://www.mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/blgg/gg/2025/art/2025/art_5e663d0bfec7452dabd950d0f0e42153.html), OFAC licenses [U](https://ofac.treasury.gov/system/files/2026-03/iran_glu_0.pdf) and [X1](https://ofac.treasury.gov/media/936446/download?inline=), and the [fixed BLS September 3 release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/prod2_09032026.htm). Their narrow dates and scope remain as recorded above. This does not claim a new review of every other reference in this document, current sanctions eligibility, an official transcript, or verification of the creator's audio.
