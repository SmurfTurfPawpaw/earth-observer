# Intelligence source policy

Reviewed 11 October 2026. The panel discovers public reports and links to their original sources. It does not copy article bodies, photographs, maps, or publisher summaries, and it does not certify reported claims. No user photos or notes are sent to these providers.

## Baseline integrations

- **The GDELT Project — DOC 2.0 Article List.** [Official API documentation](https://blog.gdeltproject.org/gdelt-doc-2-0-api-debuts/) documents JSON article discovery and multilingual searches. [GDELT's terms](https://www.gdeltproject.org/about.html) permit unrestricted academic, commercial and governmental use, including redistribution, with a project citation and website link. The panel retains article-title/link metadata and names the publisher and GDELT. Publisher content rights are separate; article pages and images are never fetched or republished. `seendate` is shown as **indexed**, not silently promoted to the original publication time. Country searches match the country's name in coverage; publisher country is separately labeled and is not an event location. Category assignments are discovery topics, not validated classifications.
- **U.S. Geological Survey — M4.5+ earthquakes, past seven days.** [Official GeoJSON feed documentation](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/v1.0/geojson.php) describes origin, update and generation times and links to individual event reports. [USGS copyright guidance](https://www.usgs.gov/information-policies-and-instructions/copyrights-and-credits) explains the public-domain status of USGS-produced information and exceptions for credited third-party material. Only USGS measurement metadata is used here, with attribution; no third-party photographs or documents are copied. Origin time is shown as an event time, review status can change, and this feed is omitted under a country filter because it supplies no authoritative country codes.
- **Global Disaster Alert and Coordination System, GDACS — recent disaster event metadata.** The [official API quick start](https://www.gdacs.org/Documents/2025/GDACS_API_quickstart_v1.pdf) states that data are free through its APIs and requires source acknowledgement. The [March 2025 terms](https://www.gdacs.org/Documents/2025/GDACS_Terms_of_use_Mar_25.pdf) explain that modeled or automatic notifications may contain errors, may need validation, and do not replace alerts issued by national authorities. Metadata carries the GDACS attribution and retains reported event times, modification time, location and affected-country codes. Locations may be representative centroids. Every GDACS card identifies it as a modeled/automatic report, and links to the original event report.

## Request and freshness behavior

Nothing is polled at startup. Opening the panel reads the project's scheduled GDELT discovery snapshot plus applicable official hazard feeds. The [snapshot job](GDELT_SNAPSHOTS.md) makes three fixed global topic queries at least six seconds apart, at most every 30 minutes. It publishes only when all queries and metadata checks succeed, preserving the prior release on failure or rate limits. Visitors share one fixed release acquisition, capped at 1 MiB and cached for 30 minutes. An optional persistent store preserves the last good snapshot across Worker instances. Snapshot generation, provider retrieval and article indexing times remain distinct; old snapshots are labeled and withheld beyond 24 hours of generation age.

Country and custom-topic filters inspect only headline metadata within this bounded scheduled sample. They do not issue additional GDELT searches or promise comprehensive coverage. A country's name in a title is a mention, not an event location; publisher country is separate. Empty filtered results do not establish that no reports or events exist. The response and source rows state these limitations explicitly.

USGS caches for five minutes and GDACS for ten minutes. Concurrent identical requests coalesce; acquisition counts, response bodies and cache keys are bounded. Failures trigger provider backoff and preserve explicitly stale last-good results, subject to the 24-hour limit. `GDELT_LIVE_FALLBACK=1` is an operator-controlled extension used only when the scheduled snapshot is unavailable. Its live requests retain six-second provider spacing and exponential backoff, including the explicit HTTP 429 reason and upstream `Retry-After` instructions. This extension is absent in the public baseline.

Only an opened, visible panel refreshes. Hidden tabs and closed panels abort browser requests and suspend refresh timers. Topic input is bounded and does not expose arbitrary upstream query operators or URLs. News searches cover 24 hours; hazard queries cover seven days. These are bounded samples and cannot establish complete global coverage.

## Related coverage

Grouping uses close earthquake coordinates/time or similar headline words/time, and retains each original title and link separately. The group label is **indicative related coverage**, never confirmation or a confidence score. GDACS and USGS may share measurements; articles from different domains may be syndicated or use the same underlying source. Search matches and grouped links must not be interpreted as independent corroboration.

## Deferred integration

ReliefWeb is excluded from the baseline. Its [current request documentation](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/parameters) requires a pre-approved application name from 1 November 2025. Its [API terms](https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/#terms-of-service) also note that contributed reports can retain their original sources' copyrights. A future integration requires approved credentials and a metadata-only scope; no arbitrary application name is assumed to be authorized.
